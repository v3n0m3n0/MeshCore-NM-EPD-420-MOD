## Executive Summary

Release **v1.18.0-mod-rev0.2** delivers critical power-rail stability, LiPo battery protection, shared I2C reliability, and deep-sleep GPIO hold fixes across all 5 MeshCore firmware roles (`companion_radio_ble`, `companion_radio_usb`, `repeater`, `room_server`, `kiss_modem`).

## What's New in `v1.18.0-mod-rev0.2`

* **Deep-Sleep GPIO Hold Latching & SX1262 Power Cut:** Added `gpio_hold_en()` and `gpio_deep_sleep_hold_en()` on non-RTC peripheral power gates (`AHTX0_POWER_PIN` IO40, `PIN_AMP_ENABLE` IO41, `PIN_ADC_CTRL` IO43, `PIN_ES8311_POWER` IO44, `SX126X_POWER_EN` IO47), disabled false `DIO1` wakeups when SX1262 is powered off, and RTC-held `P_LORA_NSS` (`IO8`) and `PIN_SDCARD_CS` (`IO7`) HIGH.
* **Glitch-Free Wakeup Latch Release:** Updated `NMEPD420Board::begin()` to force deterministic active/idle levels on SPI CS and power lines *before* releasing `gpio_deep_sleep_hold_dis()` / `gpio_hold_dis()`, eliminating wakeup SPI CS glitches.
* **Safe I2C Startup Sequencing (Anti-ESD Clamping):** Powers on `PIN_ES8311_POWER` (`IO44`) and `AHTX0_POWER_PIN` (`IO40`) before `ESP32Board::begin()` initializes the shared `SDA`/`SCL` bus (`IO39`/`IO38`), preventing parasitic ESD diode clamping to GND (~0.6 V) while keeping `PA_CTRL` (`IO41`) muted.
* **LiPo Discharge Cutoff & Divider Drain Fix:** Enforced `AUTO_SHUTDOWN_MILLIVOLTS=3300`, `BATT_MIN_MILLIVOLTS=3000`, `BATT_MAX_MILLIVOLTS=4200`, and switched `PIN_ADC_CTRL` (`IO43`) LOW immediately after each 20 ms settle + 8-sample read cycle in `readRawBattMilliVolts()`.
* **Native AHT20 I2C Calibration (`0xBE`):** Replaced Adafruit AHTX0's `0xE1` init sequence with direct `Wire` transactions (`0x38`) issuing `[0xBE, 0x08, 0x00]` calibration and `[0xAC, 0x33, 0x00]` measurement triggers reliably across cold power-cycles and UI forced refreshes.
* **Windows Flasher Improvements:** Added UTF-8 console output and `tool-esptoolpy` discovery in `flash.ps1` and fixed delayed-expansion menu selection in `flash.bat`.
