## Executive Summary

Release **v1.17.1-mod-rev0.3** introduces asynchronous Core 0 E-Paper rendering, 8 MB Octal PSRAM queue allocation, non-blocking ADC/AHT20 state machines, shared HSPI microSD CS bus isolation, enhanced Unread message pagination, and restored PlatformIO definitions for all 5 board roles on MeshCore v1.17.1. *(Internal milestone `v1.17.1-mod-rev0.2` was folded directly into this release.)*

## What's New Since `v1.17.1-mod-rev0.1`

* **Asynchronous Core 0 FreeRTOS E-Paper Task & 16 MHz FSPI:** Offloaded `GxEPD2` SPI transfers, BUSY waits, and `display.powerOff()` to `epdRefreshTask` on Core 0 and raised FSPI clock to 16 MHz (`<1 ms` main-thread commit latency).
* **$O(1)$ Circular Buffers & 8 MB Octal PSRAM Offline Queue:** Moved `MyMesh::offline_queue` (256 frames, saving ~44.5 KB internal SRAM) into external PSRAM and expanded `SerialBLEInterface::send_queue` to 16 frames with $O(1)$ circular indexing.
* **Non-Blocking Sensor & Battery Warmup:** Converted runtime `ADC_EN` (`IO43`) and `AHT20` (`IO40`) refreshes to two-stage `millis()` state machines with 15 s sensor caching.
* **Shared HSPI Bus MicroSD Guard (`PIN_SDCARD_CS` `IO7`):** Explicitly drives and RTC-holds `GPIO 7` HIGH across active and deep-sleep modes to prevent microSD bus contention with SX1262 LoRa.
* **Bidirectional Unread Navigation & Extended UTF-8:** Added `Unread: X/Y` pagination (`BOOT` older / `USER` newer), $O(\log K)$ binary-search ellipsizing, `0xC2` symbol transliteration (`°`, `±`, `²`, `³`, `µ`, `·`, NBSP), visual 5 s factory reset prompts, and restored all 5 PlatformIO environments in `variants/nm_epd_420_bw/platformio.ini`.

## Preserved Baseline Foundation (`v1.17.1-mod-rev0.1`)

* Native `400x300` (`200x150 @ 2.0x`) E-Paper canvas with `CRC32` frame cache, static `"now"` timestamps (`<60s`), `powerOff()` after updates, full refresh every 30 partials, `NM_DY` vertical centering, and 8-row `RECENT` view.
* UTF-8 OOB guard in `translateUTF8ToBlocks()`, 15 s adaptive TWDT (`esp_task_wdt_reconfigure()`), `USER` (`IO45`) BLE bond NVS clear, UART `IO43/44` pin collision cleanup, `BOOT`/`USER` polling race guard, 161-byte Unread buffer, 12-point LiPo OCV curve, and `EXT0`/`EXT1` (`BOOT IO0` / `DIO1 IO14`) deep-sleep wakeup.
